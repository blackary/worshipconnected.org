### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

How many offenses can you overlook? A handful? Two? None?

When Adam and Eve committed the first sin, could God have punished them with death right away? In one sense, yes. God had prescribed the death penalty for that sin, and it would be just for Him to extinguish mankind at that point and not have to be sinned against anymore. But in another sense, no, God could not have done it. God decreed from eternity all that should happen, including the way of salvation from the original sin and all subsequent sins. Therefore, the Eternal, true to His own will, prolonged human history, so that all of His people could find salvation in Jesus. This means that God chose to patiently forbear His rightful anger against our sins for millennia, that you could repent and believe in Christ's righteous sacrifice (Rom. 3: 23–26).

How longsuffering has God been toward you?
