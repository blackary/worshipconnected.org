### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Can you think of situations in your life where you have had to find a healthy balance between eagerness and patience?

Let me give you a few examples. (1) Students heartily desire the end of the school year, leading to vacation, yet they normally wish there was more time to finish their final assignments. (2) Your wedding date approaches, and you desire to move into your new house as soon as you can, but you realize there is flooring, plumbing, and painting yet to be finished. (3) You desire to see your firstborn grow and reach all the marks, but as he does, you miss holding your little baby.

When the apostle Paul faced a somewhat similar dilemma, he found the key for Christian contentment in his maxim "to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain" (Phil. 1: 21). While he desired to go to the Lord soon, yet he was happy to continue to live a life of service to the church. May you too find your satisfaction in doing the will of God, while hoping for that day when you will see your Lord's face in glory.
