### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

IFTTT. If this, then that. Computer geeks think like that all the time. If you are a computer geek, then you think often in terms of IFTTT. But such a formula isn’t just for computer geeks. It’s for Christians, too. Paul calls the Philippians to consider what they have received from their salvation and how they respond to such good things.

What is the 4-fold “if” condition? Encouragement in their union with Christ. Comfort in being loved by Christ. Fellowship with Christ by the Holy Spirit. Affection and mercy from Christ Himself.

Since these things are true for the Philippians, and for you Christians too, what is the 3-fold “then”? Harmony, humility, and helpfulness. If you had to describe this in one word, humility would be the word. So, having called the saints to humility, Paul shows them what true humility looks like using the perfect example, our Lord Jesus. Christ—the eternal Son of God lived this humble life. Then, Christ died—a painful, unfair, agonizing, obedient death. For us—us Christians. But He didn’t stay dead! He didn’t stay dead!

Because Jesus emptied Himself and died an obedient death, God raised Him from the dead and raised Him up to the very highest place. If you have bowed your knee before Him, then raise Him up! Raise Him up in how you live. Tell others of your great and awesome Savior. And, have in yourself the mind of Christ.
