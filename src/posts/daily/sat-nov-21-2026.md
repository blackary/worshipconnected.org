### Notes

(See below for all authors.)	

Who decided which books go in the Bible? Some have suggested the church fathers, some the pope, some Constantine. The Westminster Confession reminds us that the authority of Scriptures *dependeth not upon the testimony of any man, or church; but wholly upon God (who is truth itself) the author thereof.*" Calvin helpfully clarified that *the same Spirit, therefore, who has spoken through the mouths of the prophets must penetrate into our hearts to persuade us that they faithfully proclaimed what had been divinely commanded*. Warfield remarks that *the canon of the New Testament was completed when the last authoritative book was given to any church by the apostles*.

What a joy and comfort to know that we have access to each and every word Jesus desired to speak to our hearts! Our duty is not to pick and choose Scriptures, but rather to heed to the voice of the Spirit speaking out of the holy writings into sanctified hearts. You have received the treasure of Scriptures, faithfully handed down and preserved. Fill your mind with these saving words\!
