### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Like in Luke 9:1-2, Jesus sends out His disciples to go and preach. This time He sent seventy-two other disciples (Bently notes that *there is some dispute about whether the text says seventy or seventy-two were sent out at this time, but the number makes little difference to their work and its significance*). They were to go in pairs as laborers in His harvest. Again they were to travel light. And again they were to shake the dust off their feet as they left any town that rejected the message of the kingdom. And the Lord granted them success in their mission!

But those who refuse to listen to Jesus’s disciples are refusing to listen to Jesus. And to reject Jesus is also to reject the Father. Mason notes that *Jesus was saying. Men and women can reject the ideas of other people with impunity, but when we reject God’s Messiah we put our souls in jeopardy. The stakes are high when we hear the Christian gospel and when we open a Bible. We have a choice—not whether we like the preacher or the literary style of the Bible, but whether we will reject or accept the message*.

How do you respond to the gospel message?
