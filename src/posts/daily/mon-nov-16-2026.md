### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

Jesus asked His disciples who **_the crowds_** thought He is and then asked them who **_they_** thought he is. Peter answered that *You are the Christ (or Messiah) of God*. And we find much about this Messiah of God in the *My Servant* passages in Isaiah. God’s Servant—is God’s Messiah—is Jesus.

Here in vs 1-6 God the Father discusses with God the Son that to send God the Son to simply save the Jews is too small a task. No, God says, *I will also make you a light for the nations, to be My salvation to the ends of the earth*. The Jews needed a Savior, to be sure! But we hear in this secret eternal counsel that the whole world needs a Savior and God will send Jesus to be that Savior of the world. Although Jesus was sent primarily to the Jews (Mt 15:24), He would send His disciples to be His witnesses to all nations (Mt 28:18).

This is who God’s Servant is. This is who the Messiah of God is. As Ortlund comments, *Jesus Christ is the Savior of the world, and there is no other. He is God’s appointed “light for the nations.”* If not, then we would remain in darkness forever.
