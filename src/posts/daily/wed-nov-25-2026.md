### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

If you read Habakkuk's prayer in Habakkuk 3, how many references to previous Old-Testament events could you find there? (Some are more obvious, others quite obscure.) The prophet looked at the Scriptures he had and summarized them in the word “salvation” (Hab. 3: 13, 18). To that, 2 Peter agrees, when the apostle remarks that the words of the prophets and the commandments of the apostles concern "the Lord and Savior" (2 Peter 3: 2). Reading Scriptures is a constant exercise of learning about and from Jesus and so becoming closer to him. He gave you his very words. Fill your mind with them. Hide this treasure in your heart.
