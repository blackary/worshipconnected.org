### Notes

(See Saturday for authors.)	

No matter what accomplishments we might have in this life, none is better than being saved. The seventy-two rejoiced on returning from their missionary travels and rejoiced in the success the Lord had given them. But Jesus rejoiced that God had revealed His truths to these disciples who had humbled themselves like a little child (Matt 18:2-5). 

As Anyabwile puts it, *it’s as if God with his own fingers has opened our eyelids and given us sight. He opened our eyes to see Jesus. He removed the scales and glaucoma that blocked our sight of Christ. He touched us and caused us to see. We were once blind and now we see. What made the difference? God revealed himself to us through his Son, and the Son showed us the Father. If you can see God through faith, you ought to rejoice*.

Has God opened your eyes to know and believe His gospel? Is He continuing to reveal His wonders to you as you hear the Word preached and as you read it in private and family worship? Like those seventy-two and the twelve and all others who truly followed Jesus, we are blessed greatly to see and believe these glorious gospel truths.
